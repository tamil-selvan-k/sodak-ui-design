Privacy Policy

How SoDak EduTech collects, uses, and protects the information you share with us.

Last updated: 1 September 2026

SoDak EduTech ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website at sodakedutech.in and delivers campus training programs to engineering colleges in Chennai and Tamil Nadu. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and what rights you have over your information.

By using our website or submitting an enquiry form, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not submit any information through our website.

1. Information we collect

We collect information that you provide directly when you interact with our website. This includes:

  • Name: provided via the enquiry or contact form.
  • Email address: provided via the enquiry form, newsletter subscription, or direct email.
  • Phone number: optionally provided via the contact form or WhatsApp contact button.
  • Institution or organisation name: optionally provided if you are a Training and Placement Officer (TPO) or faculty member making an enquiry.
  • Message content: the text you write in the enquiry or contact form.

We do not collect payment information, government ID numbers, or any sensitive personal data through our website. We do not use tracking pixels, third-party advertising cookies, or behavioural analytics tools.

We may collect limited technical data automatically — such as your IP address, browser type, and referring URL — for security and spam-prevention purposes. This data is not used for advertising or profiling.

2. How we use your information

We use the information you provide for the following purposes:

  • Responding to enquiries: When you fill out the contact form, we use your name, email, and message to respond to your question about our training programmes.
  • Program logistics: If you or your institution proceeds to book a training programme, we use your contact details to coordinate session scheduling, trainer assignments, and post-programme feedback collection.
  • Newsletter and blog updates: If you subscribe to our blog updates, we use your email address to send you new posts. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send.
  • Improving our website: Aggregate, anonymised usage data (pages visited, time on page) may be used to improve the website's content and user experience.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making, profiling, or to build inferred datasets about you. We do not sell or rent your personal information to any third party.

3. Who we share your information with

We do not share your personal information with third parties for commercial or marketing purposes. Information may be shared in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: We use a small number of trusted technology service providers — including email hosting and form submission tools — that process data on our behalf. These providers are contractually obligated to use your data only to provide the services we have engaged them for.
  • Legal obligations: We may disclose information if required to do so by law, court order, or regulatory authority in India.
  • Business transitions: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or transfer of our business, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you via email before such a transfer occurs and your data becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

Outside of the above, we do not disclose your personal information to any external party.

4. How long we retain your information

We retain enquiry and contact form submissions for a period of 24 months from the date of submission, unless a longer retention period is required for ongoing programme coordination. Newsletter subscription data is retained until you unsubscribe.

You may request deletion of your personal information at any time by writing to us at hello@sodakedutech.in. We will delete your information within 30 days of a verified deletion request, except where retention is required by applicable law.

5. Cookies

Our website uses only essential cookies required for the website to function correctly (for example, to maintain form state or prevent duplicate form submissions). We do not use advertising cookies, retargeting cookies, or third-party analytics cookies such as Google Analytics.

You may disable cookies in your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect the functionality of some features on the website.

6. Children's privacy

Our website is intended for training and placement officers, faculty, and adult students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through our website, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, or loss. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal retention requirements).
  • Withdraw consent to receive marketing or newsletter communications at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority if you believe your rights have been violated.

To exercise any of these rights, write to us at hello@sodakedutech.in with the subject line "Privacy Request". We will respond within 30 days.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of our website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
