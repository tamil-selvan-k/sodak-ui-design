About SoDak EduTech

A training company that trains the way real companies hire

Founded in Chennai. Built by engineers. Backed by experience at Amazon, Zoho, Accenture, TCS, Northern Arc Capital, PayPal, and Juspay.

Our Story

Built because campus training was broken

Engineering students were graduating with three or four years of classroom theory but no real handle on what a hiring manager at Amazon, Zoho, or TCS would actually ask in round one. The gap wasn't motivation — it was access. Access to engineers who'd recently cleared those rounds, who knew exactly what the filter questions were.

SoDak EduTech was founded to close that gap. We send practising engineers into campuses — not retired professors or career trainers — to deliver training that's tightly scoped to current hiring patterns and exit-criteria verified through our own platform.

What we believe

Principles we don't compromise on

🎯

Practitioners only

Every trainer is an active engineer at a tech company. Not a career trainer. Not someone who last wrote code in 2019. If they can't pass the interview themselves today, they don't train for it.

📊

Exit criteria, not attendance

We measure completion by competency benchmarks, not seat time. Our own assessment platform verifies that students actually moved the needle — not just that they showed up.

🔬

Honest about scope

We don't sell a 3-day workshop as a career transformation. Training is one input. We're explicit about what we can and can't do, and we build programs accordingly.

⚙

Infrastructure we own

We built our own CTF platform, LMS, and assessment engine. So training quality doesn't depend on third-party platforms going down or changing their pricing.

🤝

Campus-first model

We go to the campus — not the other way around. The batch comes as-is, not self-selected. That's harder for us, and better for placement outcomes.

📍

Chennai-rooted

We know the companies hiring in Tamil Nadu, the colleges here, the hiring patterns, and the cultural context of the students. That local knowledge is a real advantage.

Journey

How we got here

2023

First campus program delivered

A 30-student batch at St. Joseph's College of Engineering — placement prep, hands-on labs, real interviews as the final checkpoint.

2024

CTF Platform and LMS launched

Built our own infrastructure — CTF challenges, LMS with embedded assessments, and 100-seat proctored exam engine — to stop depending on third-party tools.

2025

Expanded to 6+ colleges, 6 stacks

Added cloud, AI/ML, DevOps and cybersecurity tracks. Now covering the full hiring spectrum from MAANG to service companies.

2026 →

200+ students trained. Scaling across Tamil Nadu.

Entering partnerships with placement cells directly, adding corporate & FDP tracks, and expanding the trainer network.
