About the role

SoDak EduTech delivers technology training inside engineering colleges across Chennai. Our trainers are practising engineers — not career educators — and that's what makes our sessions different. Students don't just hear concepts; they hear war stories, debugging stories, and honest hiring-round stories from engineers who lived them.

We're looking for a Cloud & AWS trainer who can bring this to our campus programmes on a part-time basis. You'll run workshops, labs, and Q&A sessions that go beyond slides and consoles, and into the real reasoning behind architectural decisions.

What you'll be responsible for

  • Delivering live, lab-based AWS sessions to batches of 30–80 students at partner engineering colleges
  • Running hands-on exercises using our pre-provisioned AWS lab environment (students access via browser — no setup friction)
  • Adapting session pacing to batch readiness — no two batches are identical
  • Participating in a brief pre-session call with our program team to align on student context and prior knowledge
  • Providing session feedback to help us improve the curriculum after each batch
  • Optionally contributing to our assessment question bank (compensated separately)

What you'll teach

  • AWS core services: EC2, S3, RDS, IAM, VPC, Route 53, CloudFront
  • Compute and serverless: Lambda, ECS, API Gateway
  • Storage and databases: DynamoDB, ElastiCache, EBS
  • Security fundamentals: IAM policies, KMS, Security Groups, GuardDuty
  • Deployment and infrastructure: CloudFormation, Elastic Beanstalk basics, cost management
  • Interview preparation: how AWS questions appear in TCS Digital, Amazon, and Zoho hiring rounds

Who decides the curriculum?

We provide a base syllabus for each programme, scoped around the batch year, branch, and placement targets. Trainers are encouraged to supplement with their own real-world examples, current projects (without proprietary details), and first-hand hiring experience. The curriculum is yours to own within our guardrails.

Requirements

Who we're looking for

We don't require a teaching background. We require depth, honesty, and the ability to make hard things clear.

🏢

Working at a tech company

You're currently employed as an engineer, architect, or senior technical role at a product or services company. You bring current, not historical, context to the classroom.

☁

Hands-on AWS experience

You use AWS regularly in your work — not just certification knowledge, but real production experience. You've debugged IAM policies, designed VPC architectures, and set up Lambda functions that actually run in production.

🗣

Strong communication

You can explain complex technical concepts to a room of undergraduates without losing them or boring them. Bonus: you've mentored interns, led internal talks, or run community sessions before.

📅

Availability 1–2 days/week

You can reliably commit to one or two sessions per week across a 4–8 week programme. You give advance notice if your calendar changes. Consistency matters to the students who plan around your sessions.
