Features

Everything trainers and students need

No bloated feature lists borrowed from enterprise learning platforms. Six focused capabilities that make campus delivery smoother.

🛠️

Course Builder

Trainers create structured courses with modules, resources (slides, PDFs, code), and embedded assessments. Drag-and-drop ordering, publish/unpublish per module.

📈

Progress Tracking

Per-student completion tracking across modules. Trainers see who is behind, who completed everything, and where the batch is spending the most time.

✅

Embedded Assessments

Quizzes and graded assessments sit directly inside the course flow — no separate login, no redirect to another platform. Results feed into the batch analytics dashboard.

📊

Batch Analytics

Aggregate view of the batch: completion rates, average scores per module, time-on-platform, and assessment performance trends. Exportable for TPO reports.

🖥️

Trainer Dashboard

Single-view control panel: active batches, student progress, upcoming assessments, and support requests. Trainers can post announcements and schedule content releases.

📱

Mobile-Friendly

Fully responsive — works on Android phones, tablets, and college lab PCs. Content loads efficiently even on 4G connections typical of college hostel Wi-Fi.
