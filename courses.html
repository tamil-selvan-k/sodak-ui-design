How It Works

Institution-gated access

Courses are licensed at the institution level — not by individual students. This keeps quality high and cohorts aligned.

1

Institution Licenses

Your college's Training & Placement Office or CSE/IT department licenses a course domain bundle from SoDak. A single license covers the entire eligible batch.

2

Students Get Access

Once your institution licenses a course, all enrolled students of that institution are automatically granted access — no individual purchases or codes required.

3

Track on the LMS

Student progress, completion rates, and assessment scores are tracked on the SoDak LMS dashboard — visible to both the student and the institution's coordinator.
