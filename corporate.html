Our Approach

What makes it different

Most corporate and FDP providers use the same slide decks they've used for years. We don't.

🧑‍💻

Taught by practitioners

Every trainer actively works in the domain they teach — at Amazon, Zoho, TCS, Accenture, Northern Arc Capital, PayPal, or Juspay. No career trainers. No slide readers.

⚡

Aligned to current tech stack

Content is built around what's being deployed in production today — not textbook examples or 3-year-old AWS screenshots. Labs reflect current console UI and current service offerings.

📊

Outcomes tracked

For corporate programs: pre and post assessments, skill gap closure report, and trainer feedback included. For FDPs: completion certificate with lab hours logged per participant.

Engagement Process

From enquiry to delivery in 4 steps

We keep the sales process short and the scoping thorough — you should know exactly what you're getting before signing off.

1

Scope Call

30-minute call to understand your team size, current skill level, target outcomes, and schedule constraints. No sales pitch — just scoping.

2

Proposal

We send a structured proposal within 48 hours: program outline, day-by-day agenda, trainer profiles, assessment plan, and pricing. One revision included.

3

Delivery

Program delivered on agreed dates — onsite, remote, or hybrid. Participants get lab access, course materials, and daily summaries. Trainer available for post-session Q&A.

4

Reporting

Post-program report with attendance, pre/post assessment scores, skill gap closure metrics, and trainer observations. Certificates issued to all qualifying participants.
