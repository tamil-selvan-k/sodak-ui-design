Life at SoDak

What it's like to train with us

We set the bar high — and we make it genuinely worth your time.

📅

Flexible schedule

Sessions are planned around your work calendar, not the other way around. We coordinate with the college TPO in advance and confirm your slots at least two weeks out. Weekend and evening sessions available.

🎯

Real impact on students

Our trainers regularly hear from students who cleared interviews at companies they once aspired to. The feedback loop is direct and personal — it's not abstract volunteering. You'll know when your session made the difference.

💰

Competitive trainer fee

We pay a per-session trainer fee that reflects the quality we expect and the expertise you bring. Rates are discussed transparently during onboarding. We never underpay for quality instruction.
